# Why this ensemble

Earlier synthetic-map studies were run before the final routing geometry correction.

v2.82 repeats the spatial-heterogeneity question using the 8-neighbor formulation that is now the numerical default.

Two terrain pairs are used:

- hard vs loose: strong contrast;
- dense vs loose: deformable-soil vs deformable-soil contrast.

Three compositions distinguish sparse, balanced, and dominant occurrence of terrain B.

Random vs clustered arrangements test whether the same overall composition can produce different route opportunities.

Ten seeds are used to avoid basing conclusions on one particular generated map.

None of these generated maps is claimed to represent a statistical distribution of Mars or lunar terrain. They are controlled test ensembles.
